How it works

  1. 1Choose a category

    Everyday stories, big words, proverbs, numbers, code, or any text you paste in.

  2. 2Choose your time

    Pick 15 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 or 2 minutes, or type in a custom time.

  3. 3Type and check your result

    Correct letters turn dark, mistakes turn red, and a vine grows as the clock runs. Then see your speed and accuracy.

Choose your practice

  • Everyday storiesShort, easy passages.The library opens at nine.
  • Big wordsSentences full of long words.A remarkable constellation.
  • ProverbsShort sayings, full sentences.Practice makes perfect.
  • NumbersTimes, prices and facts.The train leaves at 7:45.
  • CodeBrackets, arrows and quotes.items.filter(n => n > 3);
  • Your own textPaste anything and type it.Your homework, a song, a story.
